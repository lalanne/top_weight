Top Weight

The gist

  • Top Weight works fully offline by default. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on cloud backup.
  • Cloud backup is optional. Turning it on creates an account (email + password) and syncs your family's profiles, exercises, and workout history so a new phone can pick up where the old one left off.
  • We don't run ads, sell data, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • You can permanently delete your cloud account and everything backed up to it, any time, right in the app.

1. Information we collect

What we collect depends entirely on whether you've turned on cloud backup. If you haven't, we collect nothing — the app has no server to send data to.

Email address & password Only if you create a cloud account Password is handled by our authentication provider; we never see or store it in plain text.
Profile names & avatar choice Local always; cloud if backup is on E.g. "Dad," "Mia" — whatever names you enter for the people you train with.

Custom profile photos Local only, always Camera or photo library pictures never leave your device, even with cloud backup on.

We don't collect location, contacts, browsing activity, advertising identifiers, or any device data beyond what's needed to run the app.

2. How we use it

The only reason we process anything beyond your device is to run cloud backup: signing you in, storing your family's records, and restoring them when you sign in on a new device. We don't use your information for advertising, profiling, or share it with data brokers.

3. Where it's stored

On your device: always, regardless of whether you have an account — Top Weight stores everything locally using Apple's on-device database.

In the cloud: only if you create a cloud account. We use Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider, to host that data. Access is restricted at the database level so that only your own account can ever read or write your family's rows — no other user or account can see your data.

4. Your choices

  • Stay fully offline. Cloud backup is opt-in — the app is complete and permanent-storage-safe without ever creating an account.
  • Access or export your data. Contact us at [email] and we'll help.
  • Delete your account. In the app: Cloud Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything backed up to it. Data still on your device isn't affected — uninstall the app to remove that too.

5. Children's information

Top Weight is built for a parent or guardian to log workouts for themselves and their children under their own account. Children don't create separate accounts, sign in, or provide information to us directly — if you add your child's name and workout data, you're doing so as their guardian, within your account.

6. Data retention

Cloud data is retained until you delete your account or specific records. On-device data is retained until you delete it in the app or uninstall Top Weight.

7. Security

Data sent to the cloud is encrypted in transit. Database access is scoped per-account so only you can reach your family's rows. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Top Weight after a change means you accept the update.
