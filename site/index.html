Questions about Top Weight

Answers to the most common questions, and how to reach us if yours isn't here.

How do I log a workout for myself and my kids in the same session?

On the Record tab, pick a user from the top menu, fill in the set, and save. Switch to the next family member with one tap — the exercise you had selected stays the same, so you don't have to re-pick it for each person.

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Top Weight works fully offline with no account at all — every profile, exercise, and workout is stored on your device. Creating a cloud account is entirely optional and only needed if you want your data backed up and restored on a new phone.

How do I back up my data, or restore it on a new phone?

Open the Users sheet (the "+" next to User on the Record tab) and go to Cloud Account. Creating an account there automatically uploads everything already on your device. Signing into that same account on a new phone restores it.

How do I delete my account and cloud data?

Users sheet → Cloud Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and everything backed up to it. Workouts already stored on your phone aren't affected — they stay local unless you also delete them or uninstall the app.

How do I edit or delete a logged workout?

On the History tab, tap a row to edit it, or swipe left for edit/delete options.
